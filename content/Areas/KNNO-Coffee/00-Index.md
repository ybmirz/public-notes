## Glossary (quick)

- **Co-ferment** — coffee deliberately fermented with added inputs (yeast, fruit, microbes) to amplify flavour.
- **Anaerobic** — fermented in oxygen-free sealed vessel; tends toward funkier, fruitier cup.
- **Carbonic Maceration (CM)** — whole cherries fermented in CO₂-rich environment, borrowed from wine.
- **Light roast** — first-crack-completed-only-just; preserves origin character, requires precise extraction.
- **Co-roasting** — renting time on a shared commercial roaster (e.g. CoRoasting KL).
- **DTM (Dev-Time Ratio)** — minutes after first crack ÷ total roast time. Light roasts ~15–22 %.
