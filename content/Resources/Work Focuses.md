## How to use this with your daily note

1. **Morning**: Open today’s daily → pick 1–3 focuses and add tasks under each.
2. **During the day**: In “Work notes by focus”, jot what you did or decided when you switch context; link to the notes above when it becomes a proper note.
3. **Evening**: Move any “work notes” that deserve a full note into the right project/area folder and link from the daily.

Your daily template already has sections for each focus; this page is the map to the actual project and area notes.
